Progressive overload

Each strength exercise shows your previous week's sets and recommends what to try next. Reach the top of the rep range with good form before increasing weight. These are suggestions, not requirements.

Log each set as you train. Empty fields mean unrecorded. For dumbbells, enter the weight of one dumbbell consistently. Never force a heavier lift on a fatigued day.

Simple daily rhythm

Small breakfast · ~20–30 g protein

Protein shake + bagel or Greek yogurt. Pre-cooked breakfast sausages are an option when you enjoy them.

Lunch · ~30–40 g protein

Costco chicken burrito bowl, or a pre-cooked protein plus rice, beans, salsa and vegetables.

Dinner · ~35–45 g protein

Protein pasta Alfredo with pre-cooked chicken or white beans, peas and spinach. Or a curry with tofu and rice.

Evening snack

Popcorn stays. Add Greek yogurt or a protein drink only if needed to reach your goal.

Protein amounts are illustrative, not product-specific nutrition facts. Read labels and adjust portions.

Your standby food system

Designed around avoiding raw-meat prep and reducing food waste.

Freezer

Frozen cooked chicken, vegetable mixes, edamame, curry portions, burrito bowls.

Pantry

Protein pasta, Alfredo ingredients, beans, rice, curry paste, popcorn.

Fridge

Protein shakes, Greek yogurt, tofu, pre-cooked proteins. Freeze extras before they spoil.

Two meals worth repeating

Easy protein Alfredo: cook protein pasta; warm an Alfredo sauce with frozen spinach/peas, and stir in pre-cooked chicken or beans. Add pasta water to loosen.

Low-effort burrito bowl: rice, black beans, pre-cooked chicken or tofu, frozen peppers, salsa and a little cheese.
